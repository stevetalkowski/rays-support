Privacy Policy

RAYS for iPhone · Effective November 2026

In short: RAYS doesn't collect any information about you. Everything you do in it stays on your iPhone.

What RAYS collects

Nothing. RAYS has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. We, Sketchbot Studios, Inc., receive no data from the app.

Your files

Models you open, scenes you save and images you render stay on your device, in Files › On My iPhone › RAYS, unless you send them somewhere yourself (for example to Photos, Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). RAYS only reads the models you open; it never changes them.

Photos

RAYS asks for permission to add images to your photo library, so it can save your renders there. It cannot see or read your existing photos.

Settings

RAYS remembers your preferences (such as render quality and your Preview Effects look) on your device only.

Children

RAYS collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
